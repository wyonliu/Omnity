# Interview with Bob (2026-03-25)

Bob is the engineering director. He has been with the company for seven
years and currently manages three teams: harness, infra, and ingest. Bob
approved the v0.9 roadmap on 2026-04-02. He is known for his insistence on
"tests before merge" — no PR lands without passing CI.

Bob's backup plan if the harness project slips past Q2 is to spin the
skills.sh packaging work out into a separate public library and let the
runtime catch up to it later. He'd prefer not to, but he's said it out
loud twice in the last month.
