# Interview with Alice (2026-03-20)

Alice is the product lead of the Harness Runtime initiative. She joined the
project in February 2026, reporting to Bob. She keeps a weekly Thursday
design review and has written three proposals so far: context-first memory,
skill packaging, and overnight sync.

Alice's favourite tool is `ripgrep`. She still prefers writing code in Rust
even though the project is mostly Python, because "Rust makes bad ideas
harder to express". She dislikes lengthy status meetings; if a meeting runs
past 30 minutes she leaves and writes up the remainder asynchronously.
